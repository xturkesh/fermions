## 1. Chronology of claims and methods (who claimed what, with which method, at which sizes)

### Takeaway
The debate opened with Cao-Tilloy-De Luca (arXiv 2018, SciPost 2019), who argued from a quasiparticle picture that arbitrarily weak monitoring of occupations destroys the unitary volume law. Alberton-Buchhold-Diehl (arXiv 2020, PRL 2021) then reported numerically a BKT-like transition at finite gamma_c, from an "extended critical" log-law phase with gamma-dependent effective central charge to a Zeno-like area law. Buchhold-Minoguchi-Altland-Diehl (PRX 2021) backed this with a replica Keldysh/bosonization RG. Numerics from 2021 to 2023 were split: Coppola/Karevski/Collura found area law at any rate, while Szyniszewski-Lunt-Pal and Lóio et al. read their data as BKT. From 2023 the nonlinear-sigma-model (NLSM) theory of Poboiko-Pöpperl-Gornyi-Mirlin reinterpreted the whole regime as a crossover. By 2025-2026 independent field theories, numerics up to L = 16384 and the original proponents' own follow-up all agree there is no transition on the clean free line.

#### 2018-2019: Cao, Tilloy, De Luca (the "no transition" starting point)
- arXiv:1804.04638 (early title "Entanglement in a Free Fermion Chain under Continuous Monitoring") was published as SciPost Phys. 7, 024 (2019), doi:10.21468/SciPostPhys.7.2.024: received 10 June 2019, accepted 8 August 2019, published 23 August 2019 — [SciPost submission page](https://scipost.org/submissions/1804.04638v5); [SciPost PDF](https://scipost.org/SciPostPhys.7.2.024/pdf); [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.04638v5).
- Model and method: a 1D free-fermion chain whose local occupation numbers are continuously measured; analysis by generalized hydrodynamics and a quasiparticle-pair picture, checked against numerics. Claim: the volume-law entanglement of unitary dynamics disappears under even very weak continuous measurement, with good agreement between the picture and the numerics — [DOAJ record](https://doaj.org/article/101b185bc9e549cfbbde03ae9cf821c8); [ar5iv](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1804.04638).
- Later papers treat this as the start of the field. Niederegger et al. (2025/2026) open with "a study of the entanglement entropy of the quantum trajectories of a free fermion chain under continuous monitoring of local occupation numbers by Cao, Tilloy, and De Luca" — [arXiv:2510.19459](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19459). An excerpt of the Alberton et al. text presents their numerics as a challenge to an earlier quasiparticle-picture conclusion that there is no transition — [arXiv PDF 2005.09722](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.09722).

#### 2020-2021: Alberton, Buchhold, Diehl (the transition claim)
- arXiv:2005.09722 was first titled "Trajectory dependent entanglement transition in a free fermion chain" and was revised in July 2021. It was published as "Entanglement Transition in a Monitored Free-Fermion Chain: From Extended Criticality to Area Law", PRL 126, 170602 (2021), doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.126.170602 — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.09722); [APS](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.126.170602); [Köln repository](https://kups.ub.uni-koeln.de/56761/).
- Setup: free fermions hopping on a periodic chain with continuous monitoring of local densities (quantum state diffusion), simulated exactly as Gaussian trajectories. Claims: under weak monitoring, "a novel dynamical regime of subextensive entanglement growth, reminiscent of a critical phase with an emergent conformal invariance"; under strong monitoring, "a transition into a quantum Zeno-like area-law regime" — [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.09722).
- Quantitative content (from excerpts): there is a transition "from the logarithmic scaling behavior, with a gamma-dependent effective central charge, to an area law at a finite gamma_c". c(gamma), and with it the log scaling, vanishes above gamma_c for L -> infinity. The finite-size analysis assumes a BKT-type correlation length xi ~ exp(alpha/sqrt(|gamma - gamma_c|)) — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2005.09722); [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.09722). The BKT identification rested on logarithmic finite-size corrections near the critical point — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.09722).
- The v1 abstract adds that the transition appears only when probability is conserved exactly along each trajectory; when it is conserved only on average, the transition is absent or pushed to infinity — [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.09722v1).
- An appendix value gamma_c ~ 0.2 belongs to a monitored unitary-circuit variant. It is described there as non-universal, depending on the rate of the unitary disorder, and is not the main continuous-monitoring result (excerpt only) — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.09722).

#### 2021: Buchhold, Minoguchi, Altland, Diehl (field theory backing the transition)
- arXiv:2102.08381 was published as PRX 11, 041004 (2021), doi:10.1103/PhysRevX.11.041004 — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.08381v3); [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/30c4f6be59044d4ba5e08018f1edde87).
- Method: locally measured Dirac fermions in 1+1D map at long wavelengths onto a monitored interacting Luttinger liquid. In an n-replica Keldysh field theory, one set of degrees of freedom "heats up indefinitely" while the other n-1 reduce to an effective non-Hermitian Hamiltonian. The RG gives a gapless critical phase with log entanglement growth and a gapped area-law phase, separated by a BKT transition — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.08381v3).
- Quantitative: in the gapless phase the effective central charge (the coefficient of log L) depends on the measurement rate, and numerics confirm a power-law decay c(gamma) ~ gamma^(-kappa). The weak-monitoring behaviour is described as non-universal — [APS PDF](https://link.aps.org/pdf/10.1103/PhysRevX.11.041004).

#### 2020-2021 context results
- Fidkowski, Haah and Hastings, Quantum 5, 382 (2021), doi:10.22331/q-2021-01-17-382, arXiv:2008.10611, show that free-fermion systems (quadratic Hamiltonians, measurements of fermion bilinears) purify in a time quadratic in system size, so "a volume law phase for the entanglement entropy cannot be sustained in a free fermion system" — [Quantum](https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2021-01-17-382/).
- Chen, Li, Fisher and Lucas, PRR 2, 033017 (2020), arXiv:2004.09577, study random non-unitary free-fermion circuits in 1D. Their steady states show logarithmic violations of the area law and power-law correlations, matching a 2D CFT with spacelike time; they argue this is generic for free non-unitary dynamics with time-dependent randomness — [APS](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.2.033017); [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.09577).
- Fuji and Ashida, PRB 102, 054302 (2020), arXiv:2004.11957, treat interacting bosons under continuous position measurement, not free fermions. They find TLL-like features in the symmetry-resolved entanglement, which they attribute to U(1) symmetry — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.11957).

#### 2021-2023 numerics and phenomenology
- Coppola, Tirrito, Karevski, Collura, arXiv:2109.10837, cited as PRB 105, 094303 (2022) (journal ref seen only in citing literature): random projective measurements in a non-interacting spinless-fermion chain change the time growth of entanglement from linear to logarithmic; stationary entropies and their fluctuations are analysed with the quasiparticle picture — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.10837); [ar5iv](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2109.10837).
- Their 2024 proceedings (Karevski, Coppola, Tirrito, Collura, arXiv:2412.06440, title spelled "Measurments-induced quantum phase transitions") state that "the volume-law phase is absent for any measurement rate". In the stationary regime the log scaling does not survive the thermodynamic limit, leaving a single area-law phase at any finite rate, "excluding the possibility of an intermediate logarithmic phase at finite measurements rate" — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.06440v1). CONFLICT NOTE: one search digest summarized the same proceedings as reaching "a final logarithmic phase at finite measurements rate". The explicit conclusion quoted above supports the area-law reading — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.06440).
- Carollo and Alba, PRB 106, L220304 (2022), arXiv:2206.07806 (arXiv title "Entangled multiplets and unusual spreading of quantum correlations in a continuously monitored tight-binding chain"; v1 June 2022, v2 December 2022), argue that the quasiparticle-pair picture does not fully capture correlation spreading. Multipartite correlations point to entangled multiplets, and monitoring can stabilise entanglement where unitary dynamics would leave subsystems unentangled — [Tübingen record](https://publikationen.uni-tuebingen.de/xmlui/handle/10900/139710?show=full); [arXiv](https://export.arxiv.org/abs/2206.07806).
- Ladewig, Diehl and Buchhold, PRR 4, 033001 (2022), arXiv:2203.00027, add dephasing. They combine exact small-system solutions, trajectories and a replicated Keldysh theory, and report that "the measurement-induced phases survive environmental dephasing". They also find a new strongly mixed phase with a decoherence-induced temperature and gapless classical diffusion — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.00027); [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prresearch/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.4.033001).
- Szyniszewski, Lunt and Pal, PRB 108, 165126 (2023, Editors' Suggestion, published 17 Oct 2023), arXiv:2211.02534, add a random potential. Their clean limit "fully reproduce[s] existing results". The critical phase is stable up to finite disorder, with criticality consistent with BKT; phase boundaries come from data collapses of the half-chain entropy and effective central charge at L = 128, 196, 256, 384, 512 and 768. Monitoring counteracts localization — [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevB.108.165126); [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.02534); [ORA](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid:e7fb24ae-cb24-4335-94be-88b68bfbaacc).
- Lóio, De Luca, De Nardis and Turkeshi, PRB 108, L020306 (2023), arXiv:2303.12216. Majorana (Z2) case: in the mixed phase the purification time grows as tau_P ~ L ln L, with a finite residual entropy for 1 << t << tau_P. Dirac (U(1)) case: the purification time is sublinear at any measurement rate, and the model shows an "apparent" BKT criticality — [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevB.108.L020306); [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.12216).

### Inferences
- Early work split along method lines. Phenomenological quasiparticle approaches (Cao et al. 2018/2019; Coppola et al. 2021/2022) never found a finite-rate transition. Groups that fitted finite-size data to BKT-type ansätze (Alberton 2020/2021; Szyniszewski 2022/2023; Lóio 2023 for purification) did, and all of them used L <= ~10^3, below the L ~ 10^4 that Fan et al. later found necessary (Section 6).
- In the field-theoretic picture, the v1 observation of Alberton et al. that the transition needs exact probability conservation along trajectories is the statement that Born-rule weighting (replica limit R -> 1) matters. This is consistent with later replica-limit analyses (Section 2) but was not an explicit argument in those later papers.

### Gaps
- The main-text value of gamma_c/J in Alberton et al., their maximum L and their trajectory counts could not be retrieved; only the appendix circuit value gamma_c ~ 0.2 and the BKT ansatz were seen.
- The maximum L in Cao et al., Coppola et al., Lóio et al. and the Buchhold PRX lattice checks was not retrieved.
- The exact stationary-state formula in Cao et al. (for example how S scales with J/gamma) was not retrieved. The common shorthand "area law for any gamma > 0" fits their abstract-level claim, but its exact wording was not seen.

## 2. Nonlinear sigma model and replica field theory of the U(1) case

### Takeaway
Poboiko-Pöpperl-Gornyi-Mirlin (PRX 13, 041046, 2023) derived, from a Keldysh plus replica treatment of random projective n_i measurements, an NLSM with a diffusive replica-symmetric S^2 sector and a replica-asymmetric 2D SU(R) sector with R -> 1. At Gaussian level it gives log growth of C_2 and S. The one-loop RG makes the log saturate at S ~ (J/gamma)^2 beyond l_corr, with ln l_corr ~ J/gamma: an area law and no transition, with an exponentially large crossover scale. Independent derivations in other microscopic settings reach the same conclusion: Fava-Piroli-Bernard-Nahum (PRR 2024; large-flavour SU(N) NLSM plus hydrodynamics), Guo-Foster-Jian-Ludwig (PRB 2025), Starchl et al. (fermion counting, PRX Quantum 2025) and Niederegger et al. (arbitrary unraveling phase, PRB 2026). The contrasting Majorana case without U(1) has an orthogonal NLSM with a stable (ln L)^2 phase.

### Cited Findings

#### Poboiko, Pöpperl, Gornyi, Mirlin, "Theory of free fermions under random projective measurements"
- arXiv:2304.03138 (v3 dated 3 Dec 2023) was published as PRX 13, 041046 on 8 Dec 2023, doi:10.1103/PhysRevX.13.041046 — [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prx/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevX.13.041046); [ADS](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2023PhRvX..13d1046P/abstract); [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03138); [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/bb4f18b880324c0f87b3dc2de73bac5e).
- Regime and method: rare measurements, gamma/J << 1, where gamma is the measurement rate per site and J the hopping. Keldysh path integral with the replica trick, leading to an effective NLSM — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03138).
- Target space: the replica-symmetric sector is a U(2)/U(1)xU(1) ~ S^2 sigma model with diffusive behaviour. The replica-asymmetric sector is a two-dimensional NLSM on the SU(R) manifold in the replica limit R -> 1 — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03138); [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.03138).
- Predictions: at Gaussian level, logarithmic behaviour of the second cumulant C_2 of the subsystem particle number and of the entanglement entropy. The one-loop RG shows that "this logarithmic growth saturates at a finite value ~(J/gamma)^2 even for rare measurements, which corresponds to the area-law phase. This implies the absence of a measurement-induced entanglement phase transition for free fermions". The crossover occurs "at exponentially large scale, ln l_corr ~ J/gamma" — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03138).
- On numerics: the crossover is "very sharp as a function of the measurement frequency gamma/J, which can be easily confused with a transition from the logarithmic to area law in finite-size numerical calculations" — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03138).
- A figure in the paper compares S/C_2 with a dashed line at pi^2/3 (the first term of their cumulant expansion) in the ballistic regime l << l_0 (figure-caption excerpt) — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.03138).

#### Same group: higher dimensions, interactions, non-commuting measurements, dynamics
- Poboiko, Gornyi, Mirlin, PRL 132, 110403 (2024), arXiv:2309.12405 (v3 18 Mar 2024). In d > 1 there is a transition between a gapless phase with l^(d-1) ln l scaling of C_2 and S and an area-law phase. Method: replica SU(R) NLSM in d+1 dimensions with R -> 1, and a one-loop RG in d = 1 + epsilon for the critical indices. Numerics in d = 2 locate the critical point and give nu ~ 1.4 — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.12405v3); [KIT copy](https://publikationen.bibliothek.kit.edu/1000170046/177570809).
- Poboiko, Pöpperl, Gornyi, Mirlin, PRB 111, 024204 (2025), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.111.024204, arXiv:2410.07334 (submitted 9 Oct 2024, revised 10 Jan 2025). With interactions "there are measurement transitions in any dimension, unlike free fermions, which always remain in the area-law phase". The interaction-induced NLSM terms lower the action's symmetry, so free fermions carry an extra symmetry that interactions break — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07334).
- Poboiko, Szyniszewski, Turner, Gornyi, Mirlin, Pal, PRL 135, 170403 (2025, published 21 Oct 2025), arXiv:2501.12903. With non-commuting, U(1)-preserving measurements on neighbouring sites (misalignment theta), an effective NLSM with long-range couplings produces Lévy flights of entanglement and fractal scaling S ~ l^(1/3) at theta = pi, in an intrinsically short-ranged free-fermion model — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12903v3); [KIT TKM page](https://www.tkm.kit.edu/english/publications/4301_4367.php).
- Poboiko and Mirlin, PRB 113, 144311 (2026, dated 17 Apr 2026), doi:10.1103/w1yq-xxbk, arXiv:2512.01772 (posted 1 Dec 2025; published title "...scaling at measurement-induced phase transitions"). The analytical framework tracks how correlations evolve from initial-state values to the steady state. The "localization" time equals both the purification time and the charge-sharpening time, and the problem maps onto quasi-one-dimensional localization with T* ~ xi_loc/v — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01772); [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/w1yq-xxbk); [APS PDF](https://journals.aps.org/prb/pdf/10.1103/w1yq-xxbk).

#### Fava, Piroli, Bernard, Nahum, "Monitored fermions with conserved U(1) charge"
- arXiv:2407.08045 (v1 title "A tractable model of monitored fermions with conserved U(1) charge"; v3 a minor revision) was published as PRR 6, 043246 (December 2024) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08045); [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/fbb02d98732142b6b81b1db27d5ca11b); [arXiv v1 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.08045v1).
- Model and method: a multi-flavour 1D chain with random complex hoppings and continuous monitoring of the local density. The replica trick is used with the number of flavours as the large control parameter, following their earlier Majorana approach. The effective theory is an SU(N) replica NLSM (the "entanglement sector") coupled to fluctuating hydrodynamics of the conserved density — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08045); [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/fbb02d98732142b6b81b1db27d5ca11b).
- Result: the bipartite entanglement entropy obeys an area law at all monitoring rates, with nontrivial scaling when the correlation length is large, in contrast with non-interacting Majorana fermions without U(1). According to one abstract snippet the derivation "clarifies some vexed points in the recent literature"; a second search could not confirm that phrase — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08045); [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/fbb02d98732142b6b81b1db27d5ca11b).

#### Contrast case without U(1): Fava, Piroli, Swann, Bernard, Nahum (Majorana)
- PRX 13, 041045 (2023), doi:10.1103/PhysRevX.13.041045, arXiv:2302.12820 (v1 24 Feb 2023, v2 8 Dec 2023). A multiflavour Majorana chain with continuous monitoring of local fermion parity is described by an NLSM for an orthogonal N x N matrix, with N -> 1; a higher-symmetry NLSM applies on one boundary of the phase diagram. In the nontrivial phase, an asymptotically exact calculation gives S ~ (ln L)^2, and transitions out of that phase are vortex-unbinding transitions — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.12820); [APS](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevX.13.041045).

#### Guo, Foster, Jian, Ludwig, "Field theory of monitored, interacting fermion dynamics with charge conservation"
- arXiv:2410.07317 was published as PRB 112, 064304 (August 2025), doi:10.1103/gdxd-pw8v, and presented at APS March Meeting 2025 (session N29) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07317); [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/gdxd-pw8v); [APS meeting](https://meetings-archive.aps.org/smt/2025/mar-n29/10/).
- A replica Keldysh field theory for monitored fermions with charge conservation. The non-interacting 1D case has only an area-law phase and no measurement-induced transition. Interactions lower the replica symmetry and allow more phases and transitions, and the theory explains why a charge-sharpening transition should appear only in the volume-law phase — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07317); [arXiv HTML v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.07317v2).
- A related later paper, arXiv:2510.23706 (PRB, Editors' Suggestion, doi:10.1103/874y-tk4t; author list only partly seen, including a "Matthew S."), monitors the local density of interacting fermions and builds the entanglement action from a trace-log expansion around the measurement-induced saddle point. It conjectures that the MIPT belongs to the non-interacting DIII class, with the volume-law phase arising through a "dangerously irrelevant" mass — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.23706v1); [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/874y-tk4t).

#### Other replica/NLSM works on 1D U(1) monitoring
- Starchl, Fischer, Sieberer, PRX Quantum 6, 030302 (2025, published 2 Jul 2025), doi:10.1103/jppz-vdgn, arXiv:2406.07673 (submitted 11 Jun 2024, revised 12 Feb 2025). Free fermions exchange particles with reservoirs and each exchange is counted. A high counting rate speeds up fluctuations rather than freezing the state ("generalized Zeno effect"), and steady-state correlations and entanglement closely resemble those under occupation measurement. NLSM analysis: no critical phase with log entanglement and conformal invariance, but a finite range of length scales where conformal signatures are visible. The results do not depend on particle-number conservation — [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/abstract/10.1103/jppz-vdgn); [arXiv](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2406.07673).
- Niederegger, Vovk, Starchl, Sieberer, PRB 113, 144317 (2026), doi:10.1103/636v-6s73, arXiv:2510.19459. An unraveling phase phi interpolates between stochastic unravelings of the same Lindblad equation: at phi = 0 measurements disentangle sites, while at phi = pi/2 they act like random unitary noise and give volume-law steady states. A replica Keldysh NLSM describes the long-wavelength physics, and "our simulations confirm these predictions, establishing the absence of measurement- or unraveling-induced entanglement transitions in this model" — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19459); [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/636v-6s73).
- Müller, Buchhold, Diehl, "Monitored interacting Dirac fermions", arXiv:2502.02645 (4 Feb 2025, revised 21 Feb 2025; no journal version found). This is a Keldysh plus replica treatment of the Thirring model under weak density monitoring, mapped to a bosonic replica theory and analysed with the RG. With attractive interaction there is a BKT-type transition at gamma_c. In the non-interacting limit gamma_c -> 0, "reducing the algebraic phase to a single point". On the free line, entanglement computed from density-density correlations shows "no entanglement transition at nonzero measurement strength in the thermodynamic limit" — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02645); [ar5iv](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2502.02645).
- Chahine and Buchhold, arXiv:2309.12391 (journal not confirmed), treat 2D U(1) monitored fermions with a replica quantum master equation, giving either a (2+1)D SU(R) NLSM or, on bipartite lattices, a non-Hermitian SU(2R)-symmetric Hubbard model — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.12391).
- arXiv:2302.09094 (Majorana circuits; author list NOT confirmed by the searches, recalled as Jian, Shapourian, Bauer, Ludwig). Born-rule and forced measurements share the same Altland-Zirnbauer class but give different universality classes: forced measurements fall into an Anderson-localization class, Born-rule ones into a novel class — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.09094).
- Giachetti and De Luca, arXiv:2306.12166 (v2 Oct 2023; journal not confirmed), study a large-N replica model of a monitored system, not a free-fermion chain. The order of the transition depends on the replica number (first order at n = 3, second order at n = 2). Taking the thermodynamic limit first and then n -> 1+, there is no phase transition — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.12166); [ar5iv](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2306.12166).

### Inferences
- The two abstract-level numbers fit one simple one-loop structure. If the SU(R) coupling g ~ J/gamma decreases linearly in ln l, then S and C_2 ~ g_0 ln l - O(ln^2 l), which saturate once ln l ~ g_0, at S_sat ~ g_0^2 ~ (J/gamma)^2, with ln l_corr ~ g_0 ~ J/gamma. The exact prefactors (the beta-function coefficient, any 2*pi or 4*pi factor in l_corr) were NOT retrieved.
- On this structure the local slope c_eff(l) = 3 dS/d ln l should drift downward roughly linearly in ln l, so any finite-size c(gamma) estimate depends on the fit window. That fits the non-universal, L-dependent effective central charges reported in 2021-2023 (Buchhold: c(gamma) ~ gamma^(-kappa); Szyniszewski: c_eff collapses). This drift is inferred, not quoted.
- The Majorana and U(1) cases differ in the sign of the replica-limit one-loop flow. The orthogonal-target NLSM supports a stable (ln L)^2 "metallic" phase, whereas the SU(R -> 1) NLSM flows to strong coupling (area law). This is why the conservation law decides the outcome, and is inferred from the two abstracts.
- Since the Gaussian (non-renormalized) level of the NLSM already gives the log law, the earlier Abelian bosonization picture (Buchhold PRX 2021) plausibly captured that Gaussian sector but missed the replica-off-diagonal (non-Abelian) fluctuations that drive the one-loop flow. This reading is consistent with the 2025 Müller-Buchhold-Diehl result gamma_c -> 0 on the free line, but no paper was found stating it in these words.

### Gaps
- Not retrieved: the explicit one-loop beta function, the bare coupling g_0(J/gamma) with constants, and the system sizes in the Poboiko PRX numerics. Fan et al. state that earlier 1D studies were limited to roughly L <~ 2000 (Section 6).
- The explicit form of Fava et al.'s "nontrivial scaling of entanglement when the correlation length is large" (for example a ln^2 correction) and their treatment of the hydrodynamic sector were not retrieved.
- The precise target manifold and symmetry class in Guo et al., and whether the Niederegger NLSM coupling depends on phi, were not retrieved.

## 3. Is there a 2026 consensus that 1D U(1) monitored free fermions are always area law? Dissenting and nuancing works

### Takeaway
Yes, for the clean 1D chain with local density (occupation) monitoring. By late 2026 the absence of a finite-rate transition (area law for any gamma > 0, log-like behaviour only below an exponentially large l_corr) is held by several independent groups: Mirlin/Gornyi/Poboiko; Nahum/Bernard/Fava/Piroli; Foster/Jian/Ludwig/Guo; Sieberer (Innsbruck); García-García; and Collura. It covers projective, QSD/homodyne, quantum-jump, counting and continuously interpolated unravelings, and the original proponents' 2025 paper concedes gamma_c -> 0 on the free line. Remaining dissent sits in papers that add disorder, quasiperiodicity or a Stark potential (Szyniszewski 2023; arXiv:2503.23807; Zhao et al. 2025-26), contested by arXiv:2605.10758 (May 2026), and in the unraveling-induced transition of Eissler et al. (PRA 2025), contradicted by Niederegger et al. (PRB 2026). Non-area-law behaviour survives only when the problem is changed: non-commuting measurements, long-range hopping, interactions, d > 1, no U(1) (Majorana), non-Born-rule or post-selected dynamics, or monitoring of only a few sites. This consensus rests on perturbative RG plus large-scale numerics, not on a rigorous proof.

### Cited Findings

#### Consensus statements (dated)
- 2023: "This implies the absence of a measurement-induced entanglement phase transition for free fermions" — Poboiko et al., [arXiv:2304.03138](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03138).
- 2024: "area law for all monitoring rates" — Fava et al., [arXiv:2407.08045](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08045).
- 2024: "a single area-law phase for any finite measurement rate", excluding an intermediate log phase — Karevski et al., [arXiv:2412.06440](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.06440v1).
- 2024/2025: "free fermions ... always remain in the area-law phase" — Poboiko et al., [arXiv:2410.07334](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07334).
- 2025: the non-interacting 1D case has only an area-law phase and no MIPT — Guo et al., [APS PRB 112, 064304](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/gdxd-pw8v).
- 2025: fermion counting shows no critical log/conformal phase — Starchl et al., [APS PRX Quantum 6, 030302](https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/abstract/10.1103/jppz-vdgn).
- 2025: "no entanglement transition at nonzero measurement strength in the thermodynamic limit" on the free line, by the Diehl/Buchhold group itself — [arXiv:2502.02645](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02645).
- 2025/2026: "to confirm the absence of a MIPT, for both projective and homodyne measurements, predicted by the NLSM, it is necessary to reach L ~ 10000" — Fan, Yin, García-García, [arXiv:2508.18468](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18468); [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/x7vx-tqr4).
- 2025/2026: "establishing the absence of measurement- or unraveling-induced entanglement transitions in this model" — Niederegger et al., [arXiv:2510.19459](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19459).
- 2026: a paper on periodic driving (arXiv:2608.21835, authors not confirmed) states that analytical field theory and recent large-scale simulations show the apparent change is not a finite-rate transition in the thermodynamic limit, while noting that a prior study had conjectured a BKT transition at finite rate — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.21835).
- Even a 2025 paper reporting transitions in a quasiperiodic potential grants that "the absence of MIPTs in free fermions has been reported in one-dimensional tight-binding models on spatially homogeneous systems" — [arXiv:2503.23807](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.23807).

#### Dissent and "debated" characterizations
- Disorder: Szyniszewski, Lunt, Pal (PRB 2023) find a BKT critical phase stable up to finite disorder — [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevB.108.165126).
- Quasiperiodic potential: arXiv:2503.23807 (first posted 31 Mar 2025; attribution to Matsubara, Yamamoto, Koga taken from a citing paper, not confirmed) uses the Aubry-André-Harper model with QSD trajectories. It reports log -> area transitions for V < V_c, while any finite measurement stabilizes the area law for V > V_c, with V_c/J ~ 2.3 in one version — [arXiv HTML v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.23807v2); [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.23807).
- Stark and quasiperiodic potentials: Zhao, Huang, Zhang, Li, Zhong, arXiv:2509.09538 (11 Sep 2025; v4 6 Feb 2026, retitled "...in the monitored free fermion systems of localization"), use random projective measurements and report that "the Berezinskii-Kosterlitz-Thouless transition, which emerges in monitored free-fermion chains, is robust in the presence of localization". Stark-localized systems lose entanglement quickly at weak measurement and Anderson-localized ones decay more slowly — [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.09538v1); [arXiv HTML v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.09538v4).
- Rebuttal of the disorder/quasiperiodic claims: arXiv:2605.10758 (posted 11 May 2026; authors not confirmed) studies U(1) fermions with disordered or quasiperiodic potentials under homodyne or quantum-jump monitoring. It finds area-law entanglement "for any finite monitoring or disorder strength" and attributes small-size deviations to system sizes below the correlation length. It names the earlier disordered [Szyniszewski et al.] and quasiperiodic [Matsubara et al.] studies as the claims it disputes — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10758v1); [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.10758). [SECONDARY] A news write-up says it simulated up to 18,000 sites and that the critical monitoring strength is consistent with zero — [Quantum Zeitgeist](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/?p=319714).
- Unraveling-induced transition: Eissler, Lesanovsky, Carollo, PRA 111, 022205 (2025), arXiv:2406.04869 (7 Jun 2024), vary the detection scheme (alpha in [0,1]) instead of the rate. alpha = 0 gives area law, alpha = 1 volume law, and at alpha_c there is a transition from area-law to logarithmic unravelings — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04869v1); [Coventry repository](https://pureportal.coventry.ac.uk/en/publications/unraveling-induced-entanglement-phase-transition-in-diffusive-tra/). This was contradicted by Niederegger et al. (2025/2026) — [arXiv:2510.19459](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19459).
- Review language: Li, Zhong, Yu, arXiv:2503.21427 (27 Mar 2025; v1 title "...in Free Fermion Chains"), discuss disputes over whether a transition exists in 1D, and a search digest summarizes the review as saying the 1D question "remains debated". Later versions are broader, covering quantum jumps, non-Hermitian evolution and the skin effect — [arXiv v1 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.21427v1); [arXiv v3 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.21427v3).
- Methodological caution: Piccitto, Chiriacò, Rossini, Russomanno, PRB 112, 174311 (2025), arXiv:2502.09401, fit entanglement versus L over more than a decade with a function that interpolates between linear and power-law behaviour, and stress that their findings are "only based on a purely numerical analysis" — [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.09401v2); [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/3sv9-2tkw).

#### Nuances: when does non-area-law behaviour survive?
- Measured observable: non-commuting nearest-neighbour measurements that preserve U(1) give S ~ l^(1/3) at theta = pi (Lévy flights) — [arXiv:2501.12903](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12903v3).
- Conservation law: Majorana chains without U(1) have a stable (ln L)^2 phase — [arXiv:2302.12820](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.12820). Fava et al. (2024) present the U(1) area law explicitly in contrast to this — [arXiv:2407.08045](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08045).
- Interactions: transitions in any dimension — [arXiv:2410.07334](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07334); a BKT transition for attractive Thirring interactions — [arXiv:2502.02645](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02645). Xing, Turkeshi, Schirò, Fazio, Poletti (PRB 109, L060302 (2024), arXiv:2308.09133) find that interactions always go with a volume-law weakly monitored phase, non-interacting systems show sub-extensive scaling, and integrability or U(1) symmetry do not decide the phase — [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevB.109.L060302); [arXiv](https://web3.arxiv.org/abs/2308.09133).
- Dimension: a genuine transition for d > 1 — [arXiv:2309.12405](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.12405v3); [arXiv:2309.12391](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.12391).
- Long-range hopping: Minato, Sugimoto, Kuwahara, Saito, PRL 128, 010603 (2022), arXiv:2104.09118, find regimes where the MIPT survives and where it disappears even under strong measurement. For bilinear systems alpha > d/2 + 1 is a sufficient condition for the transition (alpha > d + 1 for nonintegrable systems), and numerics suggest the conditions are optimal — [arXiv](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2104.09118). Müller, Diehl, Buchhold, PRL 128, 010605 (2022), arXiv:2105.08076, find dark-state transitions with algebraic hopping, described by a modified sine-Gordon theory — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.08076).
- Measurement rule: non-unitary random free-fermion circuits show log/CFT steady states — [APS PRR 2, 033017](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.2.033017). In Majorana circuits, Born-rule and forced measurements fall into different universality classes — [arXiv:2302.09094](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.09094). Partial post-selection: Leung, Meidan, Romito, PRX 15, 021020 (2025), arXiv:2312.14022, study local-parity monitoring of free fermions (not U(1)) and find that the passage to monitored universality occurs abruptly at finite partial post-selection — [arXiv](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2312.14022); [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prx/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevX.15.021020).
- Quantum jumps and losses: Le Gal, Turkeshi, Schirò (PRX Quantum 5, 030329 (2024)) find that jumps mainly renormalize the effective non-Hermitian (no-click) dynamics, in models including a U(1) free-fermion chain; their robust sub-volume log phase is reported for the Ising chain, not the U(1) model — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.13419v3); [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/a1633e763ee04d76a53c6fb7c950f3c8). Soares, Le Gal, Schirò (PRB 111, 064313 (2025), arXiv:2408.03700) add local particle losses and tunable pairing. With pairing there is a log -> area transition that broadly matches the no-click limit; without pairing every trajectory flows to the vacuum at long times — [arXiv](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2408.03700); [IP Paris portal](https://researchportal.ip-paris.fr/en/publications/entanglement-transition-due-to-particle-losses-in-a-monitored-fer/).
- Zeno physics: strong occupation monitoring leads to a "quantum Zeno-like area-law regime" — [arXiv:2005.09722](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.09722). Fermion counting gives a "generalized Zeno effect" in which dynamics speed up rather than freeze — [arXiv:2406.07673](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2406.07673).
- Local monitoring: when density is monitored on a single site, entanglement keeps growing to an extensive value because jumps come in bunches separated by "dark intervals". Monitoring more sites gives way to the Zeno effect and the area law (arXiv:2411.13667; authors and journal not confirmed) — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.13667).
- Periodic driving: Chatterjee and Modak, arXiv:2412.01917, find in a later version an area-law phase for a symmetric drive regardless of frequency. An excerpt says that for T <= 100 the critical gamma_c, if a BKT transition exists at all, is certainly < 0.1 — [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.01917v1); [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.01917).
- Rigorous bound: free fermions purify in O(L^2) time and cannot sustain a volume law — [Quantum 5, 382](https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2021-01-17-382/).

### Inferences
- The consensus is a physics consensus (controlled perturbative RG in J/gamma plus numerics up to L ~ 10^4), not a theorem. No rigorous proof was found of area law at small gamma, or of the absence of a log phase, for the 1D U(1) chain. The only rigorous result found is the Fidkowski-Haah-Hastings bound, which excludes a volume law but cannot tell log from area law. This matters for any formalization effort: the provable statement is likely about the absence of a volume law or about bounds at large gamma, not about a "transition".
- Both the BKT ansatz (xi ~ exp(alpha/sqrt(|gamma - gamma_c|))) and the NLSM crossover (l_corr ~ exp(const J/gamma)) are essential singularities. Finite-size data at fixed L can therefore look similar under both, which explains why disorder and quasiperiodicity studies at L <= ~10^3 still report BKT, and why the 2026 rebuttal relies on much larger sizes.
- Several "transition" reports in the nuance list are not counterexamples to the U(1) area law. The Eissler alpha = 1 volume-law endpoint plausibly matches Niederegger's phi = pi/2 random-unitary-noise point, and Soares' transition requires pairing, which breaks U(1).

### Gaps
- No published responses were found from Szyniszewski/Lunt/Pal, Zhao et al. or Eissler et al. to the 2025-2026 rebuttals.
- No results were found on initial-state dependence of the stationary entanglement in the clean U(1) chain beyond purification from mixed states (Lóio et al.; Poboiko and Mirlin).
- No KPZ-type scaling claims were found for this model. The only anomalous transport/entanglement scaling found is the superdiffusive Lévy-flight case (non-commuting measurements).
- Brickwork Gaussian U(1) circuits with Born-rule measurements were not separately verified beyond Fava et al. (random complex hoppings) and the rigorous Fidkowski et al. result.

## 4. How the Alberton et al. "transition" is reinterpreted in the NLSM picture, and how the numerics are reconciled

### Takeaway
In the NLSM picture the "extended critical phase" is the intermediate-scale regime l << l_corr, where the renormalized coupling is still large, so S and C_2 grow almost logarithmically with a slowly drifting effective coefficient. The "transition" is the point where l_corr(gamma) ~ exp(const J/gamma) falls below the simulated L: a crossover that looks sharp in gamma/J and whose apparent gamma_c(L) drifts to zero as L grows, only logarithmically slowly. Apparent BKT signatures (c_eff collapses, sublinear purification times) are reproduced as crossovers. Fan et al. show L ~ 10^4 is needed to see the saturation, and the Diehl/Buchhold group's 2025 paper finds gamma_c -> 0 on the free line.

### Cited Findings
- Poboiko et al.: the crossover is "very sharp as a function of the measurement frequency gamma/J, which can be easily confused with a transition from the logarithmic to area law in finite-size numerical calculations"; ln l_corr ~ J/gamma — [arXiv:2304.03138](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03138).
- Fan, Yin, García-García: the NLSM implies that "relatively large lattice sizes are required". Earlier 1D studies were limited to roughly L <~ 2000; they reach L = 16384 (1D) and 160 x 160 (2D), and L ~ 10^4 is needed to confirm the absence of a MIPT for projective and homodyne measurement — [arXiv:2508.18468](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18468); [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/x7vx-tqr4).
- Poboiko and Mirlin (2025/2026), as summarized by search excerpts: a prior numerical study of purification time in a 1D U(1) free-fermion model read power-law scaling as a BKT transition. They argue there is no transition and that the purification time saturates at xi_loc/v at large L. The "localization" time equals the purification and charge-sharpening times, set by a quasi-1D localization problem with T* ~ xi_loc/v — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.01772); [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/w1yq-xxbk).
- Müller, Buchhold, Diehl (2025): in the non-interacting limit gamma_c -> 0, "reducing the algebraic phase to a single point in parameter space"; on the free line there is no entanglement transition at nonzero gamma in the thermodynamic limit — [arXiv:2502.02645](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02645).
- Starchl et al.: there is "a finite range of length scales where conformal signatures are observable" but no critical phase — [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/abstract/10.1103/jppz-vdgn).
- arXiv:2605.10758: deviations from the area law seen at small sizes arose because the system size was smaller than the correlation length — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10758v1).
- Karevski et al.: the steady-state log scaling is a finite-size effect — [arXiv:2412.06440](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.06440v1).
- Earlier evidence now read as crossover: the BKT ansatz xi ~ exp(alpha/sqrt(|gamma - gamma_c|)) and the vanishing of c(gamma) above gamma_c — [arXiv:2005.09722](https://arxiv.org/html/2005.09722); c(gamma) ~ gamma^(-kappa), non-universal — [APS PDF PRX 11, 041004](https://link.aps.org/pdf/10.1103/PhysRevX.11.041004); "apparent" BKT in purification — [APS PRB 108, L020306](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevB.108.L020306); BKT from collapses at L <= 768 — [arXiv:2211.02534](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.02534).

### Inferences
- From ln l_corr ~ J/gamma, setting l_corr = L gives a pseudo-critical rate gamma*(L) ~ J/ln L, which tends to zero only logarithmically. Over the size range typical of 2020-2023 studies the drift is small: ln 128 = 4.85 and ln 768 = 6.64, so gamma* would fall by only about 27% between L = 128 and L = 768, easily absorbed into a BKT fit with free gamma_c. From L = 768 to 16384 (ln L = 9.70) the expected drop is another ~32%, which is roughly the range Fan et al. needed. (The prefactor in ln l_corr is not known here, so these are scaling estimates only.)
- For L << l_corr, the NLSM's slowly shrinking coupling produces exactly the "gamma-dependent effective central charge" phenomenology of Alberton and Buchhold. These parts of the old numerics remain valid as finite-scale observations; only their extrapolation to a finite-gamma_c phase transition is superseded.
- In the NLSM picture, the purification "power laws" of Lóio et al. would be the pre-saturation regime of a time scale that ultimately saturates at ~xi_loc/v once L exceeds l_corr. This reading is an inference from the Poboiko-Mirlin excerpts.

### Gaps
- No erratum or comment by Alberton et al. on PRL 126, 170602 was found. The only explicit revision by the same authors is the 2025 Müller-Buchhold-Diehl preprint (journal status unknown).
- The explicit form in which Fava et al. "clarify vexed points", for example how the bosonization/BKT argument fails, was not retrieved.

## 5. Charge fluctuations, full counting statistics and charge sharpening as probes; entanglement and cumulant relations

### Takeaway
The subsystem particle-number second cumulant C_2 is the main analytic handle in the NLSM. Gaussian level gives C_2 and S ~ log; the RG makes both saturate, and near-Gaussian statistics give S/C_2 ~ pi^2/3 (first term of the cumulant expansion) in the ballistic regime. In free 1D U(1) monitoring, charge sharpening is not a separate transition. Guo et al. argue charge sharpening occurs only in a volume-law phase, and Poboiko-Mirlin identify the charge-sharpening time with the purification and "localization" time. Number fluctuations are also being tested as finite-size diagnostics (Torres Alegre 2026). The richer charge-sharpening phenomenology (charge-fuzzy critical phase, modified KT transition) belongs to interacting U(1) circuits.

### Cited Findings
- Poboiko et al. (PRX 2023): Gaussian-level log behaviour of the second cumulant of particle number in a subsystem and of the entanglement entropy; both saturate under the one-loop RG — [arXiv:2304.03138](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03138). The ratio S/C_2 is plotted against a dashed line at pi^2/3 in the ballistic regime l << l_0 — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.03138).
- Poboiko, Gornyi, Mirlin (PRL 2024): for d > 1 the gapless phase has l^(d-1) ln l scaling of both C_2 and S — [arXiv:2309.12405](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.12405v3).
- Fava et al. (PRR 2024): the entanglement-sector NLSM is coupled to fields describing the hydrodynamics of the conserved density — [arXiv:2407.08045](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08045); [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/fbb02d98732142b6b81b1db27d5ca11b).
- Guo, Foster, Jian, Ludwig (PRB 2025): the theory "explains why the charge-sharpening transition should appear only in the volume-law phase" — [arXiv:2410.07317](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07317); [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/gdxd-pw8v).
- Poboiko and Mirlin (PRB 2026): the "localization" time is simultaneously the purification time and the charge-sharpening time — [arXiv:2512.01772](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01772).
- Starchl et al. (PRX Quantum 2025): their generalized-Zeno and entanglement results do not depend on particle-number conservation, which the authors flag as relevant to charge sharpening in random circuits and interacting systems — [arXiv:2406.07673](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2406.07673).
- Torres Alegre, arXiv:2607.16278 (July 2026, v2 exists): trajectory-resolved correlation-matrix simulations (L <= 96, up to 128 Born-rule trajectories per point) test the particle-number fluctuation and a participation-style effective number of entangling modes as finite-size diagnostics. The findings were not retrieved — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16278).
- Interacting U(1) context: Agrawal, Zabalo, Chen, Wilson, Potter, Pixley, Gopalakrishnan, Vasseur, PRX 12, 041002 (2022), arXiv:2107.10279, introduce the charge-sharpening transition as a second transition between distinct entangling phases. Both transitions are Lorentz invariant (z = 1), and for any infinitesimal measurement rate all average Rényi entropies grow ballistically — [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/1c9447824be6499aa9fbaf57be6aa3be); [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.10279v1). Barratt, Agrawal, Gopalakrishnan, Huse, Vasseur, Potter, PRL 129, 120604 (2022), arXiv:2111.09336, give a controlled replica field theory in 1D: the charge-fuzzy phase is critical, with continuously varying exponents, and ends in a modified KT transition to the charge-sharp phase — [arXiv](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2111.09336).
- U(1) bosons: in Fuji and Ashida (PRB 2020), the symmetry-resolved entanglement shows TLL-like spectral features at measurement-induced criticality — [arXiv:2004.11957](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.11957).

### Inferences
- For Gaussian fermionic states the entanglement entropy is an exact series in the even number cumulants, with leading coefficient pi^2/3 (Klich-Levitov type expansion). The pi^2/3 line in the Poboiko figure is this leading term, so C_2 is a near-proxy for S when higher cumulants are small. This is standard background and is not quoted from the retrieved excerpts.
- Charge sharpening coincides with purification (Poboiko-Mirlin) and there is no volume-law phase (Fidkowski et al.; Guo et al.). So an interacting-circuit-style charge-fuzzy critical phase cannot persist in the free 1D U(1) chain beyond l_corr. Any apparent "charge-fuzzy" behaviour is part of the same crossover.

### Gaps
- No work was found computing higher cumulants (C_4, ...) or the full counting statistics of subsystem charge in the monitored U(1) chain beyond the C_2 and S analysis. The FCS studies known to the field (for example in the Ising chain) were not verified for this model.
- No dedicated charge-sharpening numerics for the free 1D U(1) chain were found, nor any symmetry-resolved entanglement study of the monitored free-fermion chain; Fuji-Ashida is about bosons.

## 6. 2024-2026 developments: large-scale numerics, new analytical arguments, reviews, experiments

### Takeaway
2024-2026 brought (a) consolidation of the field theory: Fava et al. 2024, Guo et al. 2025, Poboiko et al. 2025 (interacting), Müller-Buchhold-Diehl 2025, Niederegger et al. 2026, Poboiko-Mirlin 2026; (b) much larger numerics: Fan-Yin-García-García at L = 16384 (PRB 2026), and arXiv:2605.10758 at reportedly ~18,000 sites (secondary); (c) extensions to other unravelings (fermion counting; diffusive unravelings, contested), observables (Lévy flights), localization (contested), periodic driving and local monitoring; (d) a 2025 topical review that still calls the 1D question debated; and (e) no experiment specific to monitored 1D U(1) free fermions.

### Cited Findings

#### Large-scale numerics
- Fan, Yin, García-García, "Entanglement dynamics of monitored noninteracting fermions on graphics processing units", arXiv:2508.18468, published as PRB 113, 094317 (2026) on 31 Mar 2026, doi:10.1103/x7vx-tqr4. GPU methods reach L = 16384 in 1D and 160 x 160 in 2D, well beyond earlier studies (L <~ 2000 in 1D), and L ~ 10^4 is needed to confirm the NLSM-predicted absence of a MIPT for both projective and homodyne measurements — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18468); [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/x7vx-tqr4).
- arXiv:2605.10758 (May 2026): area law with disorder or quasiperiodicity under homodyne or jump monitoring — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.10758). [SECONDARY] up to 18,000 sites — [Quantum Zeitgeist](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/?p=319714).
- Niederegger et al. (PRB 113, 144317 (2026)): simulations confirm the NLSM predictions for all unravelings — [arXiv:2510.19459](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19459).

#### New analytical arguments (beyond Section 2)
- Poboiko and Mirlin (PRB 113, 144311 (2026)) give a theory of how correlations evolve in time, identify the localization, purification and charge-sharpening times, and confirm in d = 2 the analytical predictions for scaling at the transition — [arXiv:2512.01772](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01772); [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/w1yq-xxbk).
- Lévy flights for non-commuting measurements (PRL 135, 170403 (2025)) — [arXiv:2501.12903](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12903v3).
- Interacting monitored fermions: PRB 111, 024204 (2025) — [arXiv:2410.07334](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07334); Thirring (preprint 2025) — [arXiv:2502.02645](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02645); DIII conjecture (PRB, 2025/2026) — [arXiv:2510.23706](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.23706v1).
- Purification universality: Gerbino, Kim, Giachetti, De Luca, Turkeshi, "Universal purification dynamics in real non-unitary quantum processes", arXiv:2603.10751 (11 Mar 2026), study weak-measurement, volume-type phases in toy models (random Gaussian matrices; Dyson Brownian motion). They find scaling collapse in t/t_P(L) that depends on the emergent symmetry group (orthogonal vs unitary). This is peripheral to the U(1) chain; a reported Rep. Prog. #### Higher dimensions and spectral probes (context)
- Chahine and Buchhold, "Spectral Transitions of the Entanglement Hamiltonian in Monitored Free Fermions", arXiv:2509.08584 (10 Sep 2025; PRB, doi:10.1103/c954-z1pv, volume not confirmed). Short-range spectral statistics (adjacent gap ratio, KL1) detect an ergodic -> nonergodic crossover, long-range probes (spectral form factor, Thouless time) agree, and KL2 hints at a nonergodic extended (multifractal) regime at intermediate monitoring — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.08584v1); [APS](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/c954-z1pv).
- [SECONDARY] arXiv:2605.05306, "Measurement-induced phase transitions in disordered fermions" (2026, 2D, AIII class): the critical measurement strength decreases linearly with the disorder-induced decay rate. Only the title and a search-digest summary were seen — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.05306).

#### Experiments
- Searches found no experiment that realizes Born-rule monitoring of a 1D U(1) free-fermion chain, whether continuous occupation monitoring or Gaussian circuits with mid-circuit measurements. One theory paper notes that continuous monitoring corresponds to very frequent weak measurements realizable on superconducting-qubit platforms — [arXiv:2608.21835](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.21835).

### Inferences
- The two general reviews (2022-2023) predate the NLSM resolution of the 1D U(1) case (PRX December 2023), so they cannot reflect the current consensus. The only later review found (Li-Zhong-Yu 2025) still frames the 1D question as debated, which is now in tension with the 2025-2026 primary literature.
- The decisive 2025-2026 evidence comes from independent groups (Innsbruck, Shanghai/García-García, Karlsruhe/Mirlin), plus the 2025 Diehl/Buchhold paper on the free line. The open controversy has moved to inhomogeneous potentials, where the same crossover argument (L < xi) is being applied.
- Because l_corr is exponentially large in J/gamma, an experimental test of the area law at weak monitoring would need system sizes far beyond current platforms. Experiments could at most probe the intermediate log regime and its gamma-dependent slope (inference).
