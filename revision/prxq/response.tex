\noindent
\textbf{Re: QT10218, ``Non-Local Magic Resources for Fermionic Gaussian States'' --- appeal}

\medskip
\noindent
We thank you for handling our manuscript and the Referees for their careful reading. We respectfully appeal the decision and ask that the manuscript, revised as described below, be reconsidered.

\medskip
\noindent
Neither report questions the significance of the results. Referee~B finds the manuscript ``\textit{substantially strengthened}'' and states that the new Theorem~1 and its proof address their main concern. Referee~A writes that the result proved in the Appendix is, as far as they can tell, correct. The objections concern two technical points, both of which have a simple solution that leaves all the results of the manuscript unchanged:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \emph{Theorem~1} (Referee~A). For a non-contiguous bipartition, the definition of the non-local magic contains a qubit permutation $T_{\pi_{AB}}$ that is not Gaussian, so the statement of Theorem~1 was broader than what is proved. Following the Referee's suggestion, the definitions and Theorem~1 now refer to the contiguous blocks $A=\{1,\dots,\ell\}$ and $B=\{\ell+1,\dots,N\}$, for which the definition coincides with the maximization proved in the Appendix. All applications and numerical results of the manuscript use such blocks.
\item \emph{Jacobi weight} (Referee~B). The extra factor $(1-x)^{-1/2}$ in Eq.~(11) is a typo. The finite-$N$ formula is not used in any figure, the corrected one agrees with direct Haar sampling, and the thermodynamic-limit results, including the half-partition value, are unchanged.
\end{itemize}
Both points concern text that was already present, in the same form, in the first version of the manuscript, and neither was raised in the first round, as Referee~B notes explicitly. They were therefore not introduced by the revision.

\medskip
\noindent
Referee~A's remaining comments (Points~2--14) concern the presentation of the proof: requests for more explanation, wording, notation, a typo, and one intermediate line whose final result the Referee confirms. We have implemented all of them, including the shorter proof of Lemma~5 that the Referee kindly sketched, and added a paragraph explaining the logic of the proof. We have also added a statement on the use of AI tools. \todo{Keep this sentence only if the AI statement in the manuscript is confirmed.}

\medskip
\noindent
We enclose the revised manuscript, a version with all changes highlighted, and a point-by-point reply.

\medskip
\noindent
Best regards,\\
Beatrice Magni, on behalf of the Authors \todo{signature}

\begin{quote}
    \color{blue}{
    \textbf{1.} I am fairly sure that as stated Theorem 1 is false. The problem is that the $T_{\pi_{AB}}$ in equations (5) and (7) is a non-Gaussian permutation of qubits while in the appendix (e.g.\ from equation (50)) we have only Gaussian operations. Replacing the qubit swap operations with gaussian fSwap operations would kill this inequality because (for example) an fSwap can act non-trivially on qubits even when it acts trivially on the corresponding fermionic modes. Another change which would fix the theorem is to restrict to only considering contiguous partitions (so $A$ consists of the first $l$ modes and $B$ the rest).     }
\end{quote}

\reply{
We thank the Referee for this important observation and for the explicit counterexample of Sec.~3 of the report, which we have verified. We agree, and we have adopted the second fix suggested by the Referee, which preserves the upper bound $M_2^{\NLl}\le M_2^{\FNL}$ on the unrestricted non-local magic. Both Theorem~1 and, for consistency, the definitions of the non-local and Gaussian-restricted non-local magic, Eqs.~(4)--(5), now refer to the contiguous blocks $A=\{1,\dots,\ell\}$ and $B=\{\ell+1,\dots,N\}$, so that no qubit permutation $T_{\pi_{AB}}$ appears.

\medskip
\noindent
The missing step of the proof is now given below Eq.~(50): for $A=\{1,\dots,\ell\}$, the Jordan--Wigner strings of the Majorana operators of $A$ do not leave $A$, while those of $B$ differ from the ones defined within $B$ only by the parity $P_A$ of $A$. Since $U_{O_A}P_AU_{O_A}^\dagger=\det(O_A)P_A$, the qubit unitary $U_{O_A}\otimes U_{O_B}$ coincides, up to a phase, with the Gaussian unitary of the whole chain associated with $O_A\oplus\det(O_A)\,O_B$; as $(O_A,O_B)\mapsto(O_A,\det(O_A)\,O_B)$ is a bijection, Eq.~(5) is exactly the maximization of $\zeta_2$ over the orbit of Eq.~(50). We have also checked this numerically, by maximizing $\zeta_2$ at the qubit level over $U_{O_A}\otimes U_{O_B}$ for random Gaussian states. We have added a remark that the identification fails for non-contiguous bipartitions, as shown by the Referee's example. The restriction does not affect any of the applications of the manuscript: for Haar-random states and SYK$_2$ eigenstates the statistics of $\Gamma_A$ is invariant under mode permutations, while for the XY chain, the quenches, the random circuits (half chain, $A=\{1,\dots,N/2\}$) and the variational checks we use contiguous blocks $A=\{1,\dots,\ell\}$.
}

%%%%% POINT B.1
\begin{quote}
    \color{blue}{
    However, while examining the revised manuscript, I identified a separate technical issue in Sec.~III\,C concerning the finite-size distribution used for Haar-random fermionic Gaussian states. [\ldots] Equation (11) of the revised manuscript appears inconsistent with the previous result derived by Bianchi, Hackl, and Kieburg [Phys.~Rev.~B \textbf{103}, L241118 (2021)] for the same Haar ensemble of pure fermionic Gaussian states. [\ldots] Compared with Eq.~(6), Eq.~(11) of the manuscript therefore contains an additional factor $\prod_{m=1}^\ell(1-x_m)^{-1/2}$.

    I would ask the authors to reconcile Eq.~(11) with the known finite-size distribution of Bianchi et al.. In particular, I would appreciate clarification of the following points:
    \begin{enumerate}
    \item Is the additional factor $(1-x_m)^{-1/2}$ in Eq.~(11) a typographical or transcription error, or was this weight actually used in deriving the subsequent finite-size results?
    \item If Eq.~(11) is to be corrected, Eqs.~(14)--(15), including the parameters of the Jacobi polynomials and their normalization, should be revised consistently.
    \item The manuscript states that the finite-size expression obtained from Eq.~(14) is in ``perfect agreement'' with the numerical data in Fig.~1(a). [\ldots] As a direct independent check, I would also ask the authors to test at least $\ell=1$ and $\ell=2$ for several small system sizes $N\ge2\ell$. [\ldots]
    \item The authors should reassess which subsequent Page-curve results depend on the finite-size Jacobi weight. In particular, the exact finite-$N$ expressions should be corrected where necessary, and it should be checked explicitly whether the thermodynamic-limit results in Eqs.~(16)--(18), including the quoted half-partition saturation value, remain unchanged.
    \end{enumerate}
    }
\end{quote}

\reply{
We thank the Referee for spotting this inconsistency. The additional factor is a typo: the correct weight is the one of Bianchi, Hackl and Kieburg, $x^{-1/2}(1-x)^{N-2\ell}$, which we now cite. Point by point:
\begin{enumerate}
\item It is a typographical error, which also propagated to the parameters of the Jacobi polynomials and to their normalization in Eqs.~(14)--(15). No figure or number of the manuscript is computed from the finite-$N$ formula, see Point~3.
\item We have corrected Eqs.~(11), (14) and (15) consistently: $p_n(x)=\mathrm{P}_n^{(-1/2,N-2\ell)}(1-2x)/\sqrt{h_n}$, with $h_n=\Gamma(n+\tfrac12)\Gamma(n+N-2\ell+1)/[(2n+N-2\ell+\tfrac12)\Gamma(n+1)\Gamma(n+N-2\ell+\tfrac12)]$.
\item The data in Fig.~1(a) are obtained by direct sampling: $O$ is the orthogonal factor of the QR decomposition of a real $2N\times2N$ Gaussian matrix, with the standard sign fix, which is Haar distributed on $\mathrm{O}(2N)$, and $\Gamma=O\Gamma_0O^{\trans}$. The analytical curve in Fig.~1(a) is the $N\to\infty$ result, Eq.~(18); the sentence on the comparison with the finite-$N$ expression has been corrected accordingly. As suggested, we checked the corrected finite-$N$ formula against direct sampling for $\ell=1,2$ and several $N\ge2\ell$, finding agreement within statistical errors, while the weight as printed is excluded.
\item The thermodynamic-limit results, Eqs.~(16)--(18), including the half-partition value $2-\log_2(2+\sqrt3)$, are unchanged: an $O(1)$ shift of the exponents of the weight only affects the $O(1/N)$ corrections to $\overline{M}_2^{\FNL}/N$.
\end{enumerate}
}
