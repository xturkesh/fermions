Dear Dr. Regula,

Thank you for handling our manuscript QT10218, "Non-Local Magic Resources for Fermionic Gaussian States," and for forwarding the second-round reports. Before proceeding, we would like to ask whether you would consider a resubmission of a revised version.

Neither report questions the significance or the exceptionality of the results. Referee B finds the manuscript "substantially strengthened" and states that the new Theorem 1 and its proof address the main concern of the first round. Referee A, who in the first round judged the result worthy of publication in PRX Quantum provided it was proved, confirms that the result proved in the appendix is correct. The objections concern two technical details, both with a simple solution:

1. Theorem 1 (Referee A). For a general bipartition, the definition of the non-local magic includes a qubit permutation T_π, which is not Gaussian. For non-contiguous bipartitions the statement of Theorem 1 is therefore broader than what is proved. Following the referee's suggestion, we restrict the definitions and Theorem 1 to contiguous blocks, A = {1,…,ℓ} and B = {ℓ+1,…,N}. In this case the definition coincides with the maximization proved in the appendix.

2. Finite-size Page curve (Referee B). The Jacobi weight in Eq. (11) contains an extra factor (1−x)^{−1/2}, which is a typo. With the corrected weight, Eq. (11) coincides with the result of Bianchi, Hackl and Kieburg. We have corrected Eqs. (14)–(15) accordingly and checked them against Haar-random sampling, as the referee suggested. The thermodynamic-limit results, including the half-partition saturation value, are unchanged.

Both points concern expressions that were already present, in the same form, in the original submission and were not raised in the first round; Referee B notes this explicitly. They are not issues introduced by the revision, and fixing them leaves the results of the paper unchanged.

Almost all of Referee A's remaining comments (points 2–14) concern presentation: requests for more explanation of some steps of the proof, wording, notation and a typo. We have implemented all of them, including the shorter proof of Lemma 5 that the referee kindly sketched, which we have adopted. We have already prepared the revised manuscript and a point-by-point reply. Since the significance of the results is not in question, we would be grateful if you would allow us to submit them.
